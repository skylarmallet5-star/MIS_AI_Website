Cybersecurity and Online Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware attack began spreading across computer systems around the world. The malware encrypted files and demanded payment from victims to restore access to their data. This attack showed why organizations need strong cybersecurity practices, updated software, and reliable data backups.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

  1. Malware that damages or disrupts computers.
  2. Phishing emails that trick people into sharing information.

Ways to Protect Your Information

  1. Use strong passwords and multifactor authentication.
  2. Keep your devices and software updated.

Cybersecurity Best Practices

  • Avoid clicking suspicious links.
  • Back up important files regularly.
